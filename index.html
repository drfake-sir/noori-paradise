07Before you visit

The questions we hear most

A few honest answers. For anything else, message the atelier.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during opening hours and we will help you. For bridal, message ahead so the fitting room and time are kept for you.

How long should I allow?

It depends on the piece and the alterations. Tell us what you are looking for and we will plan the visit around you rather than rush it.

Do you alter and make to measure?

Yes. Fitting and alterations are part of how we work. Bring the piece back for a second fitting if it needs one — we would rather it sat perfectly.

Can I see prices before I visit?

Price depends on fabric, embroidery and fit. This demo shows price on request; in store we will show you options at the budget you name.

Do you deliver outside Chennai?

Ask the atelier when you message. Delivery and courier options depend on the piece and where it is going.

What should I bring to a bridal fitting?

Bring the shoes and jewellery you plan to wear, any inspiration photographs, and the people whose opinion matters. It makes the fitting much easier.
